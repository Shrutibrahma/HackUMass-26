# Data card (fill in during M3)

- **Signs:** list the kept vocabulary (15-25 to start).
- **Signers:** number of teammates recorded; which one is held out (reported numbers use only them).
- **Recordings per sign per signer:** target 20+.
- **Conditions:** lighting and backgrounds used; the phone on its stand at the demo position.
- **Format:** MediaPipe landmarks only (hands, upper-body pose, face subset). No video stored.
- **Checkpoint:** which pretrained model, and its preprocessing (landmark order, normalization, missing points, frame sampling).
- **Sign forms checked by:** fluent signer (name/role, with permission).
- **Known gaps:** signs or conditions that fail.
